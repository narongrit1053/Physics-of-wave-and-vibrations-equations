Topic 7.1: Forced Mechanical Oscillationsหัวข้อ 7.1: การสั่นถูกบังคับทางกลศาสตร์

  • Equation of Motion: $$m \ddot{x} + b \dot{x} + k x = F_0 \cos\omega t \implies \ddot{x} + \beta \dot{x} + \omega_0^2 x = \frac{F_0}{m} \cos\omega t$$
  • General Solution (Transient + Steady-State): $$x(t) = x_{\text{trans}}(t) + x_{\text{steady}}(t) = B e^{-\frac{\beta}{2} t} \cos(\omega_d t + \phi) + A(\omega) \cos(\omega t - \delta)$$
  • Steady-State Amplitude $A(\omega)$:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$A(\omega) = \frac{F_0 / m}{\sqrt{(\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)^2 + \omega^2 \beta^2}}$$
  • Phase Difference $\delta$:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$\tan\delta = \frac{\omega \beta}{\omega_0^2 - \omega^2}$$

Transient & Steady-State Motion Decomposition

The total response $x(t) = x_{\text{trans}}(t) + x_{\text{steady}}(t)$ transitions from high-frequency damped natural oscillations (transient) to driving-frequency oscillations (steady state).

Topic 7.2: Power Absorption, Half-Power Points & Bandwidthหัวข้อ 7.2: การดูดกลืนพลังงาน จุดครึ่งพลังงาน และความกว้างแถบความถี่ (Bandwidth)

  • Average Power Absorption: $$P(\omega) = \frac{1}{2} b v_0^2 = \frac{F_0^2 \beta \omega^2}{2 m \left[(\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)^2 + \omega^2 \beta^2\right]}$$
  • Maximum Power (at Resonance $\omega = \omega_0$): $$P_{\text{max}} = P(\omega_0) = \frac{F_0^2}{2 m \beta} = \frac{F_0^2}{2 b}$$
  • Half-Power Condition: $$P(\omega_{1,2}) = \frac{1}{2} P_{\text{max}} \implies A(\omega_{1,2}) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} A_{\text{max}} \approx 0.707 \, A_{\text{max}}$$
  • Half-Power Frequencies ($\omega_1, \omega_2$): $$\omega_{1,2} = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 + \frac{\beta^2}{4}} \mp \frac{\beta}{2} \approx \omega_0 \mp \frac{\beta}{2}$$
  • Resonance Bandwidth ($\Delta\omega$): $$\Delta\omega = \omega_2 - \omega_1 = \beta = \frac{b}{m} \quad \left(\text{or } \frac{R}{L}\right)$$
  • Quality Factor ($Q$):Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$Q = \frac{\omega_0}{\Delta\omega} = \frac{\omega_0}{\beta}$$

Half-Power Points & Resonance Bandwidth ($\Delta\omega$)

At half-power frequencies $\omega_1$ and $\omega_2$, average power drops to $P_{\text{max}}/2$ and amplitude drops to $A_{\text{max}}/\sqrt{2} \approx 0.707 A_{\text{max}}$. The bandwidth $\Delta\omega = \omega_2 - \omega_1 = \beta$.

Topic 7.3: Series RLC Circuitหัวข้อ 7.3: วงจรไฟฟ้า RLC แบบอนุกรม

  • KVL Loop with AC Source: $$L \frac{d^2q}{dt^2} + R \frac{dq}{dt} + \frac{1}{C} q = V_0 \cos\omega t$$
  • Steady-State Charge Amplitude: $$q_0(\omega) = \frac{V_0 / L}{\sqrt{(\omega_0^2 - \omega^2)^2 + \omega^2 \beta^2}}$$
  • Phase Shift:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$\tan\delta = \frac{\omega \beta}{\omega_0^2 - \omega^2}$$
  • Circuit Current: $$i(t) = \frac{dq}{dt} = -q_0 \omega \sin(\omega t - \delta)$$

Topic 7.4: Complex Impedance Analysisหัวข้อ 7.4: การวิเคราะห์ความต้านทานเชิงซ้อน (Impedance)

  • Device Impedances:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$Z_R = R, \quad Z_L = j\omega L, \quad Z_C = -\frac{j}{\omega C}$$
  • Total Impedance ($Z$): $$Z = R + i\left(\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}\right)$$
  • Impedance Magnitude: $$|Z| = \sqrt{R^2 + \left(\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2}$$
  • Phase Angle:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$\tan\delta = \frac{\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}}{R}$$
  • Ohm's Law Real Component Current: $$i(t) = \frac{V_0}{\sqrt{R^2 + \left(\omega L - \frac{1}{\omega C}\right)^2}} \cos(\omega t - \delta)$$

Topic 7.5: Electrical Resonanceหัวข้อ 7.5: การสั่นพ้องทางไฟฟ้า (Resonance)

  • Resonance Condition:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$\omega L = \frac{1}{\omega C} \implies \omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$$
  • Characteristics at Resonance:
    • Impedance Phase: $\delta = 0$
    • Impedance Magnitude: $|Z| = R$
    • Max Current Amplitude: $I_{\text{max}} = \frac{V_0}{R}$
    • Reduced Current Equation: $i(t) = \frac{V_0}{R} \cos\omega t$
