Lecture 5: LC Circuit Oscillations

Interactive Physics Visualizer: LC Circuit & Phase Relationships ($q$, $i$, $V_L$)

Topic 5.1: Differential Equations & Natural Frequency

  • KVL Loop Equation: $$-\frac{q}{C} - L\frac{di}{dt} = 0 \implies L\frac{d^2q}{dt^2} + \frac{1}{C}q = 0 \quad \left(\text{since } i = \frac{dq}{dt}\right)$$
  • Standard Form: $$\frac{d^2q}{dt^2} + \frac{1}{LC} q = 0$$
  • Natural Angular Frequency: $$\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}$$
  • Charge Solution: $$q(t) = q_0 \cos(\omega_0 t + \phi)$$

Topic 5.2: LC Circuit Energy Conservation

  • Total Electrical Energy ($E$): $$E = U_E + U_B = \frac{1}{2C} q^2 + \frac{1}{2} L i^2 = \text{constant}$$
  • Energy Conservation Proof: $$\frac{dE}{dt} = \frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{1}{2C} q^2 + \frac{1}{2} L i^2\right) = 0 \implies \frac{q}{C}\frac{dq}{dt} + L i \frac{di}{dt} = 0 \implies \ddot{q} + \frac{1}{LC} q = 0$$
