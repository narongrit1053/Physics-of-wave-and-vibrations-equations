Topic 6.1: Viscous Damping & Equation of Motionหัวข้อ 6.1: แรงหน่วงหนืดและสมการการเคลื่อนที่

  • Viscous Damping Force:แรงหน่วงหนืด: $$R = -bv$$
  • Newton's 2nd Law Horizontal Forces: $$ma = -kx - bv \implies m \frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + b \frac{dx}{dt} + kx = 0$$
  • Standard Form:Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} + \beta \frac{dx}{dt} + \omega_0^2 x = 0 \quad \left(\text{where } \omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}, \, \beta = \frac{b}{m}\right)$$
  • Auxiliary Characteristic Equation: $$p^2 + \beta p + \omega_0^2 = 0 \implies p = -\frac{\beta}{2} \pm \sqrt{\frac{\beta^2}{4} - \omega_0^2}$$

Topic 6.2: Underdamped Solution (Light Damping)หัวข้อ 6.2: การสั่นแบบหน่วงน้อย (Underdamped - หน่วงเบา)

  • Condition: $$\frac{\beta^2}{4} < \omega_0^2 \quad \text{or} \quad b < 2\sqrt{km}$$
  • Characteristic Roots: $$p = -\frac{\beta}{2} \pm i\omega$$
  • Damped Angular Frequency: $$\omega = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 - \frac{\beta^2}{4}} = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m} - \frac{b^2}{4m^2}}$$
  • Position Solution: $$x(t) = A_0 e^{-\frac{\beta}{2} t} \cos(\omega t + \phi)$$
  • Energy Decay: $$E(t) = E_0 e^{-\beta t}$$

Topic 6.3: Critically Damped Solutionหัวข้อ 6.3: การสั่นแบบหน่วงวิกฤต (Critically Damped)

  • Condition & Critical Damping Coefficient ($b_c$): $$\frac{\beta^2}{4} = \omega_0^2 \implies \beta = 2\omega_0 \implies b_c = 2m\omega_0 = 2\sqrt{km}$$
  • Repeated Real Characteristic Root: $$p = -\frac{\beta}{2} = -\omega_0$$
  • General Position Solution (Reduction of Order):Key Formulaสูตรสำคัญ $$x(t) = (C_1 + C_2 t) e^{-\frac{\beta}{2} t} = (C_1 + C_2 t) e^{-\omega_0 t}$$
  • Physical Behavior: Returns to equilibrium position in the fastest possible time without oscillating.

Topic 6.4: Overdamped Solution (Heavy Damping)หัวข้อ 6.4: การสั่นแบบหน่วงมาก (Overdamped - หน่วงหนัก)

  • Condition: $$\frac{\beta^2}{4} > \omega_0^2 \implies \lambda = \sqrt{\frac{\beta^2}{4} - \omega_0^2} > 0$$
  • Two Distinct Negative Real Roots: $$p_1, p_2 = -\frac{\beta}{2} \pm \lambda$$
  • General Position Solution: $$x(t) = C_1 e^{\left(-\frac{\beta}{2} + \lambda\right) t} + C_2 e^{\left(-\frac{\beta}{2} - \lambda\right) t} = e^{-\frac{\beta}{2} t} \left( C_1 e^{\lambda t} + C_2 e^{-\lambda t} \right)$$
  • Physical Behavior: Heavy damping prevents oscillation; the system slowly decays exponentially back to equilibrium.
