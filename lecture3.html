Lecture 3: Dynamics & Energy of Pendulums & Rotational Equations

Interactive Physics Visualizer: Animated Simple Pendulum & Energy Conservation Bar Chart

Topic 3.1: Small Angle Approximation for Simple Pendulums

  • Pendulum Equation of Motion: $$\ddot{\theta} + \omega_0^2 \sin\theta = 0$$
  • Power Series Expansion: $$\sin\theta = \theta - \frac{\theta^3}{3!} + \frac{\theta^5}{5!} - \frac{\theta^7}{7!} + \dots$$
  • Linearized Equation ($\theta \ll 1$): $$\ddot{\theta} + \omega_0^2 \theta = 0 \implies \theta(t) = \theta_0 \cos(\omega_0 t + \varphi)$$
  • Natural Angular Frequency: $$\omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{g}{L}}$$

Topic 3.2: Energy of a Simple Pendulum

  • Kinetic Energy ($K$): $$K = \frac{1}{2} I \left(\frac{d\theta}{dt}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2} I \dot{\theta}^2 = \frac{1}{2} I \omega^2$$
  • Potential Energy ($U$): $$U = mgh = mgL(1 - \cos\theta) \approx \frac{1}{2} mgL \theta^2$$
  • Total Mechanical Energy ($E$): $$E = K + U = \frac{1}{2} I \dot{\theta}^2 + \frac{1}{2} mgL \theta^2 = \text{constant}$$
  • ODE Derivation ($\frac{dE}{dt} = 0$): $$I \dot{\theta} \ddot{\theta} + mgL \theta \dot{\theta} = 0 \implies \ddot{\theta} + \frac{mgL}{I} \theta = 0 \implies \omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{mgL}{I}}$$

Topic 3.3: Rotational Inertia (Moments of Inertia $I$)

  • Parallel Axis Theorem: $$I = I_{\text{CM}} + M d^2$$
  • Thin Rod Integration Form (about end): $$I = \int_0^L x^2 \, dm = \int_0^L x^2 \frac{M}{L} \, dx = \frac{1}{3} M L^2$$

Topic 3.4: Physical Pendulum

  • Restoring Torque: $$\tau = -mgd \sin\theta \approx -mgd \theta$$
  • Equation of Motion: $$I \ddot{\theta} = \tau \implies \ddot{\theta} + \frac{mgd}{I} \theta = 0$$
  • Oscillatory Characteristics: $$\omega = \sqrt{\frac{mgd}{I}}, \quad T = 2\pi \sqrt{\frac{I}{mgd}}, \quad f = \frac{1}{2\pi} \sqrt{\frac{mgd}{I}}$$
